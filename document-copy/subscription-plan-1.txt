OUR MISSION

We are committed to simplifying our customers’ lives by providing responsive,
reliable, and professional service with a focus on trust, quality, and long-term
relationships.

Why Subscription-Based Support Is Essential in Today’s Economic Climate

In today’s economic environment, businesses across many industries are facing a
combination of slower sales cycles, tighter consumer spending, inflationary pressures,
rising labor costs, and increased expenses for materials, transportation, Fuel, insurance,
and technology. These challenges make it more important than ever for service-based and
product-based companies to create predictable, recurring revenue streams that support
long-term stability and operational goal of best service practices to try and obtaina
excellent customer experience.

Our team continues to perform many warranty-related service calls at no charge when
covered under applicable workmanship warranties or manufacturer obligations. However,
recent industry disruptions, including the bankruptcy of SunPower, have significantly
increased the volume of unsupported warranty issues and service requests that are no
longer reimbursed through traditional manufacturer channels. This has created additional
operational costs that directly impact our ability to provide ongoing service at previous
pricing structures.

While not all product or equipment warranties are covered under our service plans, we
remain committed to honoring our workmanship warranty obligations when applicable.
Coverage determinations will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis based on installation
scope, equipment manufacturer terms, warranty eligibility, and service history.
